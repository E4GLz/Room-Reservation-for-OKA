# Staff User Guide

## 1. Role Purpose

The Staff role is the standard requester role in the platform.

Staff users are expected to:

- review room availability
- submit booking requests
- follow their own booking statuses
- check their own booking history

## 2. What Staff Can Access

### Main pages

- `/dashboard`
- `/planner`
- `/bookings/new`
- `/bookings/[id]`
- `/my-bookings`
- `/profile`

### Main permissions

- create booking requests
- view planner
- view own booking history and statuses

## 3. What Staff Cannot Do

- cannot manage rooms
- cannot manage users
- cannot manage settings
- cannot access reports
- cannot directly confirm bookings
- cannot see private details of other users’ bookings

## 4. Daily Staff Workflow

1. Open the planner
2. Check room availability
3. Submit a booking request
4. Monitor status from `My Bookings` or booking detail pages

## 5. How Booking Requests Work

### Typical flow

1. Staff submits request
2. If a manager is assigned, request goes to manager first
3. After manager approval, request moves to admin
4. Admin confirms or rejects

### Possible statuses

- pending
- confirmed
- cancelled

Manager approval states may also appear for staff-created requests.

## 6. What Staff Sees In The Planner

Staff can use the planner to understand room occupancy, but not all booking details are visible.

### Expected behavior

- own bookings may be visible in more detail
- unrelated private bookings may appear as blocked
- room/date availability can still be followed

## 7. Personal Tracking

Staff can use:

- dashboard for their booking summary
- `My Bookings` for request history
- booking detail pages for individual request information

## 8. What Staff Should Expect

- Booking is request-based, not direct confirmation
- Visibility is limited to personal activity and general schedule awareness
- Admin controls rooms, approvals, and system settings

## 9. Best Practice For Staff

- Check planner availability before submitting
- Provide accurate attendees and meeting details
- Submit requests early if manager/admin approval is required
- Add food service details clearly when needed
